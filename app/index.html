Roadmap to the platinum

1 trophy may be missable — Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning and walks to the balcony of Jason's house. Follow her and accept. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends, and nobody has confirmed whether the trophy strictly needs it — do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save.

No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave.
