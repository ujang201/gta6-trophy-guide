⚠ One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy the launch guides flag as possibly missable: Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. After Chapter 1 that spot is gone, and there is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them off your trophy run.

Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

    Chapters 1–5, in order. Chapter trophies and mission trophies pop as you play and cannot be missed. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see above), do stranger strands and side missions as they appear, and scan vehicles and study animals as you travel — 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of 255 animals count toward 100%.

    Earns: all 12 chapter & mission trophies, the final-mission gold, and the five story-moment trophies.

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion (35 tasks) and work through what is left: every robbery, bank vault and gang operation, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races, Bret’s Target Decks, and 70 of the 80 challenges. Everything you did before the credits already counts.

    Earns: A Long Road (gold) and most of the activity and robbery trophies.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks about 10 hours

    Finish 8 stranger mission strands, and the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus Bret’s unique-animal hunt. None of these are part of 100%, but they are part of the platinum.

    Earns: Strangers & Freaks (silver) and the five character-final trophies.

  4. Stage 4 — Clean-up

    Finish the remaining challenges, launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all 8 garages and register 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app. When A Long Road pops, the platinum Vice Got a Price! follows at once.

    Earns: the last handful — then the platinum.

Roadmap built from the official trophy descriptions and the launch guides: PowerPyx, GTABase, Output Lag and KeenGamer (see Sources below). No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, one playthrough is enough.
